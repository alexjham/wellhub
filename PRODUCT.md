# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) on Vercel, repo `alexjham/wellhub`. Replaces the current WordPress/WooCommerce site at wellhub.com.au.

## Users

Busy Australian adults aged 25–45, often shopping for a household. They want to eat healthier without the time cost and are deciding which meal delivery service, meal kit or vitamin/supplement subscription to sign up for.

## Product Purpose

WellHub compares Australian meal delivery, meal kits, vitamin subscriptions, protein and greens powders. It helps visitors find the right service for their diet and goals, then sends them to it with a discount. Success: a visitor takes the quick quiz, gets a short personalised shortlist, and clicks through to a provider.

## Positioning

Health-first, not price-first. Listings are rated for nutrition quality by a qualified nutritionist, not only ranked by price or commission.

## Operating Context

- ~200 listings (a listing = one provider plan, e.g. "Youfoodz – Low Calorie Meal Plan") across ~30 diet/category pages.
- Each listing shows: price per serve (AUD), states delivered to (NSW, VIC, ACT, QLD, NT, SA, WA, TAS), wellness rating out of 5, a deal/code and "Go to site".
- Exclusive discount codes (e.g. WH200 Youfoodz, WELLHUBFRESH HelloFresh, HUB150 Macros).
- ~47 blog posts: nutritionist reviews, "X vs Y" comparisons, diet guides.
- Existing quiz is 9 steps and asks name and email first.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action on the new site: take the quiz.
- Revenue today is affiliate commissions and sponsored placements; affiliate links and codes will be reworked later with the owner.
- Advertiser disclosure must stay visible sitewide.
- Undecided: final category list, new categories, other revenue streams, low-tox section (parked).

## Brand Commitments

- Name stays "WellHub". Visual identity (colours, type, logo) is open for replacement.
- Current look (coral #FB9D91, charcoal, Montserrat) is evidence only, not binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real provider data, prices, codes and nutritionist reviews on the live site.
- No testimonials, user counts or press mentions are confirmed; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Health first: lead with nutrition quality, then price.
2. Fewer, clearer choices: a shortlist of 3 beats a list of 40.
3. Value before asking: show results before asking for an email.
4. Always disclose how WellHub gets paid.
